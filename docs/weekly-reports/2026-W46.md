## Decisions, supersession, and conflicts
The public decision register defines **PROPOSED → APPROVED → ACTIVE → SUPERSEDED**. A proposed policy is not binding; even an approved decision is not evidence of implementation. The initial public guidance explicitly describes candidate policies as pending approval. No verified public supersession event or additional approved/active decision was found in the reviewed sources. Private decisions were neither accessed nor inferred.

The historical November 5–10 description of limited recent context is not contradicted by the November 15 retrieval test: the later test documents an additional capability. Likewise, Bluetooth pairing success does not contradict the absence of verified audio routing. No unsupported claim of local-model installation or full hands-free functionality is made.

## Blockers and unresolved validation
- Bluetooth speaker pairing and reliable audio routing into the assistant.
- Repeatable hands-free microphone, wake-word, transcription, and speech playback.
- Conflict-aware retrieval quality and durable decision enforcement.
- Auditable decision approval, supersession, and recovery tests.
- Current backup coverage and full end-to-end application health.
- Independent confirmation that scheduled reports can be gathered and published consistently.
